# Steel Spin Race

A Fabric mod about **spin, steel, horse racing and Stands**. Throw spinning steel balls, boost your horse and awaken a spectral Stand that fights beside you.

## Features

### Steel Ball
- Hold **right click** to charge the spin (about 1.5 seconds for a full charge), release to throw.
- More spin means more speed and more damage (4 to 12 points).
- A **full-spin** hit leaves the target slowed and nauseous for a few seconds.
- The ball drops on impact so you can pick it up and throw it again.

### Race Horseshoe
- Right click a **horse, donkey or mule** to give it Speed III and Jump Boost II for 5 minutes.

### Stands
- Use an **Awakening Arrow** (right click) to awaken a random Stand. It costs 2 hearts, so you need more than 3 hearts of health.
- Press **V** (rebindable) to summon or dismiss your Stand, and **C** to use its active ability.
- Your Stand floats beside you, defends you and attacks whatever you attack. It is intangible and invulnerable.
- You keep your Stand after dying. Admins can use `/stand set <type>` and `/stand remove`.

| Stand | Style | Passive / on hit | Active ability (C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crimson Vanguard** | Heavy hitter | Strength, big knockback | **Crimson Barrage**: 3 seconds of rapid punches |
| **Tidal Echo** | Support | Regeneration + Speed, heals you on hit | **Tide Pulse**: instant heal and Regeneration II |
| **Ashen Warden** | Defender | Resistance, Weakness on hit | **Ash Shield**: extra absorption hearts |
| **Gate Warden** | Trickster | Speed | **Rift Step**: teleport behind your target (or 10 blocks ahead) |
| **Thorn Spiral** | Ranged | Haste, Poison on hit | **Spiral Volley**: fires 5 spinning shards |
| **Wild Hunt** | Beast | Speed II + Jump Boost, lifesteal | **Feral Rush**: a dash that damages nearby monsters |

## Recipes
| Item | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Steel Ball (x4) | 4 iron ingots in a cross around 1 gold ingot |
| Race Horseshoe | 7 iron ingots + 1 gold nugget (horseshoe shape) |
| Awakening Arrow | Shapeless: 1 arrow + 1 diamond + 1 amethyst shard |

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Fabric Loader 0.15+
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)

Works on both client and server. Safe to add to existing worlds.
